Common questions

How do I add the verse to my Lock Screen?

  1. Touch and hold your Lock Screen.
  2. Tap Customize, then Lock Screen.
  3. Tap the space under the clock.
  4. Choose Bible Widget, then Lock Screen Verse.

How do I add the Home Screen widget?

Touch and hold an empty area of your Home Screen, tap Edit → Add Widget, search for Bible Widget and choose Daily Verse in the size you like.

My widget isn’t showing a new verse

Open the app once. Widgets get a month of verses in advance each time you open it. If you just changed your style or translation, the widget updates within a few seconds.

Why is the Lock Screen verse shorter than in the app?

The Lock Screen has room for a few short lines, so it shows short verses whole and the well-known opening of longer ones, marked with an “a” (for example Ps 46:10a). The full verse is always in the app and on the Home Screen widget.

I bought Premium but it isn’t unlocked

Open Settings → Restore purchases in the app while signed in to the same Apple Account you used to subscribe. If that doesn’t help, send us feedback from the app and we’ll fix it.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your iPhone, open Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions, choose Bible Widget and tap Cancel Subscription. To avoid being charged, cancel at least 24 hours before your trial or period ends.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Which Bible translations are included?

The King James Version, World English Bible and Berean Standard Bible. All three are in the public domain.
