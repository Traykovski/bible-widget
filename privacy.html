Privacy Policy

Bible Widget: Lock Screen · Effective October 12, 2026

Bible Widget: Lock Screen (“the app”) is made by Atanasij Trajkovski, an independent developer (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information the app uses, why, and the choices you have. In short: most of what you do in the app stays on your iPhone, we don’t sell your data, and we don’t show ads or track you across other apps.

Information that stays on your iPhone

These are stored only on your device and are never sent to us:

  • Your preferences: translation, topics, style, notification times
  • The first name and age range you enter during setup (unless you also join our email list, see below)
  • Your saved verses and your daily streak

Daily verse notifications are scheduled on your iPhone by iOS. We don’t use push notification servers or collect a device token. Widgets read their verses from a folder shared between the app and its widgets on your iPhone.

Information we collect

WhenWhat we receiveWhy
You join our email list (optional, only with your consent) Email address, first name, age range, language/region setting, platform, app version, Support ID To send occasional offers, discounts and news about the app
You send feedback from Settings Your message and type (bug, problem, feature request, feedback), email if you add one, app version, iPhone model and iOS version, Support ID To answer you, fix problems and improve the app

Your Support ID is a random identifier created by our subscription provider. It doesn’t reveal who you are. It lets us find your subscription when you ask for help, for example to restore or extend Premium.

This information is stored with Supabase, our database provider, on servers in the United States.

Purchases and subscriptions

Payments are processed by Apple. We never see your payment details. We use RevenueCat to manage subscriptions. RevenueCat receives the anonymous Support ID, your purchase and subscription history, basic device information and, if you chose one, your age range. If you join our email list, your email is also linked to this record so we can help with purchases.

If you installed the app from an Apple Ads (App Store search ads) campaign, Apple’s AdServices framework tells RevenueCat which campaign led to the install. This is standard attribution provided by Apple, doesn’t use the advertising identifier and doesn’t track you across other apps. It helps us understand which ads bring people to the app.

Photos

When you save a verse wallpaper, the app asks for permission to add photos to your library. It cannot see or read your existing photos.

What we don’t do

  • We don’t sell or rent your personal information.
  • We don’t show ads or use advertising identifiers.
  • We don’t track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

How long we keep information

  • Email list: until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it.
  • Feedback: up to 24 months, then deleted.
  • Subscription records: as long as needed to provide Premium and meet legal and tax requirements.

Your choices and rights

You can unsubscribe from emails at any time using the link in any email or by writing to us. You can ask us to access, correct or delete the information we hold about you. Please include your Support ID (Settings → About in the app) so we can find it. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe or the CCPA in California), you may have additional rights, which we honor for all users.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has sent us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we’ll update it on this page and change the date at the top.
